A quick reading guide

Three things the numbers do—and do not—mean

A 90 is a comparison, not a 90% chance. It means the result sits above roughly 90 of every 100 players in that one role's comparison group.

Different roles use different groups. A 75 for an NBA guard and a 75 for an NFL receiver are not the same unit of sporting value.

No score shown is not zero. It means the model did not have enough familiar or reliable information to give a responsible number.
