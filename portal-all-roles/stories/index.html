Before you begin

Three numbers, three different questions

01

Cross-sport comparison

How the athlete’s body and playing profile compare with players in the selected role.

02

Build similarity

How common the athlete’s height and weight are in that role. A typical build does not mean greater ability.

03

Own-role rank

How the player actually ranked against peers in their real sport and role during the saved season.

Important: the cross-sport number is an experimental comparison, not the probability that someone could successfully change sports. The scale stops at 100; reaching that ceiling does not mean perfect ability. If the model withholds a score, that means the comparison was not reliable enough—not that the athlete scored zero. Scores from different roles are not directly interchangeable.
