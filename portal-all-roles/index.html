How to read it

Comparable does not mean equivalent.

Relative

Every number is a percentile within a specific role and reference cohort. A 75 in two roles does not imply equal sporting value.

Conditional

Missing inputs, unsupported models and out-of-domain builds produce a withheld result—not a zero.

Exploratory

Mapped performance dimensions are analogies between public statistics. They do not establish transferable skill or causality.
