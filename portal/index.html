The research

A starting point.
Not a scouting verdict.

Can physical build tell us anything useful about winning possession? This project compares athletes within sport-specific roles, then applies those models to the same height and weight across sports.

—fitted role models
—pass exploratory point checks
—statistically established improvements

The current models learn from height and weight only. Age and season performance are shown for context, not used as prediction inputs.

Each sport has its own possession and disruption target. The resulting 0–100 estimate is role-relative: it describes expected target activity among eligible elite peers, not how likely a player is to make a team.

Exploratory checks are not statistical confirmation. Held-out test samples are small, intervals can be wide, and a familiar body shape does not establish skill.

Model notes
01 / What a score means

A relative activity estimate

“Efficacy” is shorthand for the model’s role-relative possession/disruption target. It is not defensive quality, talent, a success probability or a cross-sport transfer forecast.

02 / What is missing

The athlete beyond the build

Speed, reach, limb length, technique, decision-making and spatial awareness are not learned features in this version. Listed measurements may also lag an athlete’s current physique.

03 / When we abstain

No evidence is a result

Ratings are withheld for unsupported roles, missing measurements, out-of-domain builds, failed evidence checks and data under review. No displayed score should be read as a zero.
